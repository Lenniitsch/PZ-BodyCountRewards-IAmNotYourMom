description=[h2]FAQ[/h2]
description=
description=[b]Q: Why are these traits not in the base BCR mod?[/b]
description=A: The base mod aims for lore-friendly, balanced selections. This addon is for anything goes. I am not your mom. You decide.
description=
description=[b]Q: Can other mods add traits like this?[/b]
description=A: Yes. BCR has a public extensibility API. This addon doubles as a reference implementation.
description=
description=[b]Q: Removed Short Sighted but still can't see far?[/b]
description=A: The trait is removed correctly, but the visual effect does not refresh until you reload. PZ limitation, not a BCR bug.
description=
description=[h2]About this addon[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] This addon does not change how any trait works. It just tells BCR to offer them as rewards. If a trait behaves oddly, that is a vanilla question.
description=[*] Compatible with Build 42.19+. SP and MP. Requires BCR loaded first.
description=[*] Safe to remove. Traits stay on your character. No vanilla file overwrites.
description=[/list]
